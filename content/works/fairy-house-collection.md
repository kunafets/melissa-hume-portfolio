# Fairy House Collection

## Year
2025 - 2026

## Dimensions
Approximately 12 cm tall

## Description
A collection of hand-built fairy houses in varying scales and glazes. Each house is unique, with individually sculpted roofs, doors, windows, and chimneys. The pieces range from small decorative objects to larger statement works, unified by Melissa's signature use of texture and her eye for architectural whimsy.
