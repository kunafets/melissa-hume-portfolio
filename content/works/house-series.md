# House Series

## Year
2012

## Dimensions
Heights vary, smallest approximately 12.5 cm

## Description
Hand-built ceramic houses with richly textured surfaces and coloured glazes. Each piece is unique, with individually sculpted details including doors, windows, steps, and chimneys. An early exploration of architectural form that laid the groundwork for Melissa's later sculptural work.
