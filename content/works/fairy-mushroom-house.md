# Fairy Mushroom House

## Year
2015

## Dimensions
51 cm

## Description
A hand-built sculptural mushroom house with a spotted cap and tiny doorway and windows. Finished in a warm amber and celadon glaze, the piece has a playful, otherworldly character. Designed to be displayed outdoors, it sits naturally among plants and garden growth.
